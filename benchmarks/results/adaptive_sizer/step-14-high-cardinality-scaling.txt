ADAPTIVE SIZER EXPERIMENT - STEP 14
Date: 2026-09-03
Source: Separate Criterion high-cardinality benchmark after the accepted ASCII fast path.

GOAL
Determine the input size at which the previously rejected O(n²) greedy
clustering loop becomes material to the complete optimized `count_unique_simhash`
operation.

PREDECLARED RULE
Treat greedy clustering as material when its independently measured central
latency reaches at least 20% of the independently measured full-operation
central latency. Component timings do not sum exactly, so this is a screening
threshold, not an additive CPU profile.

HARNESS
`crates/headroom-core/benches/adaptive_sizer_large.rs` is a separate Criterion
target so routine 100/1k/5k runs do not initialize large fixtures. It measures:
1. full production `count_unique_simhash`;
2. SimHash generation over the same records;
3. greedy clustering over precomputed fingerprints.

The deterministic ASCII fixture is high diversity. Before every timed group it
asserts that all items are separate representatives and that the observed
comparison count equals `n(n-1)/2` exactly.

COMMAND
cargo bench -p headroom-core --bench adaptive_sizer_large --no-default-features

INTERPRETATION
The greedy scan crosses the predeclared 20% materiality threshold between
12,500 and 15,000 distinct, high-diversity records. It is therefore still not
the best primary target for the original 100-5,000-record workloads, where the
ASCII SimHash fast path improves the dominant cost. At unusually large and
diverse payloads, exact multi-index clustering becomes a justified future
optimization candidate.

LIMITATIONS
- This is a deterministic ASCII worst case, not a representative production
  traffic sample.
- Components were benchmarked independently, so the share is a screening ratio.
- The benchmark uses 20 Criterion samples and a shorter measurement window to
  keep high-cardinality cases practical.
- Peak memory was not measured.
