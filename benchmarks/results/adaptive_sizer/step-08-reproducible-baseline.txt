INTERPRETATION
These results supersede the Step 7 latency values for submission claims because both baseline and candidate can now be regenerated from the final source. The effect is slightly smaller than the manual-toggle run but still clears the predeclared requirement of at least 10% lower latency at 1,000 and 5,000 records.

LIMITATIONS
- Saved-baseline runs were sequential, not process-interleaved.
- The workload is a synthetic homogeneous JSON array shaped like an existing coding-agent integration fixture.
- This measures Headroom's local compression route, not LLM inference or complete agent task wall time.
- No peak-memory measurement was performed.

DECISION
Use the reproducible results as the final end-to-end claim: 20.9% lower latency at 200 records, 19.3% at 1,000, and 17.4% at 5,000, with exact model-visible output equivalence.
